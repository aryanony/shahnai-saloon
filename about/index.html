About Shahnaz Beauty Parlour

Shahnaz Beauty Parlour is a unisex salon at Vishal Market, near Pillar No. 76, Sheikhpura, Raja Bazar, Patna, Bihar. The salon’s primary focus is bridal makeup and styling, alongside everyday beauty, hair and grooming services for the whole family.

The salon previously traded as Shahnai Unisex Salon — both names refer to the same salon at the same address; see that page for the full explanation.

What we focus on

  • Bridal, engagement and reception makeup, with pre-bridal services
  • Bridal hairstyling and saree / dupatta draping
  • Hair styling, facials and skin care
  • Men’s grooming

Service availability and pricing are managed directly by the salon and reflected live on the services page — nothing on this site is invented or assumed.
