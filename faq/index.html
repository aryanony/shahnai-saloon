Frequently Asked Questions

What is Shahnaz Beauty Parlour?

Shahnaz Beauty Parlour is a unisex salon and bridal makeup studio at Vishal Market, near Pillar No. 76, Sheikhpura, Raja Bazar, Patna.

Where is Shahnaz Beauty Parlour in Patna?

Vishal Market, near Pillar No. 76, Sheikhpura, Raja Bazar, Patna, Bihar 800014.

Is Shahnaz Beauty Parlour a unisex salon?

Yes. Alongside its bridal makeup specialty, the salon serves men, women and families with hairstyling, facials and grooming.

Is Shahnaz Beauty Parlour the same place as Shahnai Unisex Salon?

Yes, according to the current operator. Shahnai Unisex Salon is an earlier name for the same salon at this Raja Bazar address, now operating as Shahnaz Beauty Parlour. See the full explanation.

Does Shahnaz Beauty Parlour offer bridal makeup?

Yes — bridal, engagement and reception makeup, plus pre-bridal services. See the bridal makeup page and the services page for current availability.

Does the salon offer engagement makeup?

Engagement makeup is offered where shown as active on the services page, which reflects the salon’s current offering.

Does the salon offer bridal hairstyling?

Bridal hairstyling is available as an add-on to a bridal booking, subject to current availability.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking request form — choose your service and preferred date. It takes under a minute.

Is my appointment confirmed once I submit the form?

No — submitting the form sends a request. The salon will contact you on WhatsApp or by phone to confirm the exact time before it’s final.

How can I contact the salon?

By phone, WhatsApp, or Instagram — see the contact page for current details.

Do you serve customers from across Patna?

Yes, the salon welcomes clients from across Patna and nearby areas. All services are provided at the Raja Bazar location.

How much do services cost?

Prices are shown on the services page where available. Where a price isn’t listed, it shows as “Price on enquiry” — ask us directly and we’ll confirm it.

Can I reschedule or cancel a request?

Yes — call or WhatsApp the salon directly and mention your booking ID, name and date; there is no self-service rescheduling portal.

Does the salon accept online payment?

Not at this time. Payment is handled directly with the salon.
